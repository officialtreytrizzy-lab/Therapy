AI and wellness safety

Reviewed July 31, 2026 · Version 1.0

Do not continue a joint app exercise when you feel threatened, monitored, forced to disclose, or afraid of retaliation. Move to safety and use local emergency or trusted support resources instead.

What the Guide is

The Guide is an automated educational tool that can organize information, suggest reflection questions, and support structured conversations. It does not know everything about you, cannot verify every statement, and cannot observe physical danger or coercion outside the information supplied to it.

What the Guide is not

It is not a therapist, doctor, emergency responder, attorney, judge, investigator, or mandated decision-maker. It does not diagnose mental-health conditions, determine who is telling the truth, certify that a relationship is safe, or replace professional care.

Review before acting

Treat every AI response as a draft. Check whether it accurately reflects what you meant, whether it exposes private information, and whether following it could increase conflict or danger. Correct the record when the app gives you a correction option.

Private and shared boundaries

Use the individual space for private reflection. Share only what you intentionally choose to share. Do not copy another person's private content into a shared exercise without permission. A partner's silence, account link, or previous participation is not permanent consent.

When to stop

  • Someone is threatening violence, self-harm, exposure, eviction, financial punishment, or retaliation.
  • A device or account may be monitored by another person.
  • One participant is intoxicated, unable to consent, or being pressured to continue.
  • The conversation is escalating faster than either person can regulate safely.
  • An AI response feels accusatory, manipulative, clinically diagnostic, or dangerously specific.

Safer next steps

Pause the session. Use a device and location that are safe. Contact a trusted person or qualified local professional. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services. The app's Immediate Support screen contains additional United States resources.

Report a problem

Use the in-app reporting controls for unsafe behavior, privacy concerns, abusive content, or harmful AI output. Do not include unnecessary private relationship details in a technical report.
