Privacy notice

Effective July 31, 2026 · Version 1.0

Core promise: your private reflections are not silently turned into shared partner content. Shared actions require an intentional app flow, and server-owned records cannot be written directly from the browser.

Information the service handles

The service may handle account identifiers, email address, display name, pronouns, onboarding choices, private reflections, relationship goals, agreements, appreciations, memories, session messages, partner-link status, safety reports, deletion requests, and technical security metadata. Some information is individual-only. Other information is stored in a linked shared workspace only when the product flow identifies it as shared.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, maintain private and shared workspaces, provide requested relationship-wellness exercises, generate AI-assisted responses, prevent abuse, investigate failures, support exports and deletion, and protect the service. Relationship text is excluded from normal security logs; operational logs are designed to contain metadata rather than message content.

AI processing

When you request Guide assistance, the information needed for that request may be processed through Google Cloud Vertex AI. Prompts are designed to separate private and shared context and to avoid attributing one partner's private statement to the other. AI output can still be incomplete or wrong and should be reviewed before acting on it.

Service providers

Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, database, workload identity, and AI infrastructure. Vercel hosts the website and server functions. An email provider may deliver secure sign-in links. These providers process information to operate the service under their applicable terms and security controls.

Sharing and sale

The service is not designed to sell personal information or relationship content. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service, respond to a valid legal requirement, investigate abuse, protect a person's safety, or complete a transaction you requested.

Retention, export, and deletion

Information is retained while needed to provide the service, meet security or legal obligations, and complete requested deletion workflows. Account controls provide export and deletion actions. Shared-space deletion may require both linked members' confirmation so one person cannot silently erase the other person's shared record. Backup and provider-level deletion may take additional time.

Security

The service uses authenticated server APIs, Firestore security rules, encrypted transport, restrictive browser headers, audit metadata, rate limits, and scheduled deletion controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not enter information you would not want processed by an online wellness service.

Age and clinical status

The service is intended for adults. It is a relationship-wellness product, not emergency care, psychotherapy, diagnosis, medical treatment, legal advice, or a substitute for a licensed professional. It is not presented as a HIPAA-covered clinical service.

Your choices

You can use an individual space without linking a partner, decline shared activities, correct profile information, request an export, unlink a partner, report unsafe behavior, or request deletion. Stop using joint features when participation is coerced or unsafe.

Changes

Material changes should be reflected by a new version and effective date. Continued use after a clearly presented update may be treated as acceptance where permitted.
